# Slice 07 — Props and space

Requirement level only. Source: Game 1, Game 2, and the merge rule in the classroom-game brief. A named prop is a physical token the two games name. Pictures drawn on a note or on the shadow are not extra props. This slice says where a prop sits when the text says, and who uses it.

## 1. Scope

One classroom carries from Phase A into Phase B. Named places are the front (the desk), the back (where the kids start), the mid-way checkpoint line, the other side (the mailbox), the floor (the cushions), and a wall (the teacher’s shadow).

Named props: 22.

1. Desk
2. Guarded target (one prize; the text’s kinds are a golden trophy, a toy, or a bucket of treats)
3. Squeaky dog toy
4. Bubble wrap
5. Soft plush toy
6. Shield mat
7. Checkpoint line
8. Sticky note
9. Party popper
10. Paper confetti
11. Whiteboard
12. Computer
13. Big book
14. Secret note (the other prize)
15. Mailbox
16. Cushions
17. Whoopee cushions
18. Clown sunglasses
19. Wall shadow
20. Flashlight
21. Silly string
22. Teacher’s shoes

The beat title “The Squeaky Toy Shoes” names the under-foot walk. The objects under the feet are the squeaky dog toy or the bubble wrap. The teacher’s shoes are the finale spray target.

In scope: those 22 tokens, the places above, who uses each token, and which props Phase A leaves in the room.

Out of scope except as a handoff line: wake, freeze, laugh, strict mood, sneak and crawl, send-back, who picks an “or,” what “safe” and “gently” require in practice, the victory dance, and either finale as a session win.

Beats 3 and 9 name no physical token. Game 1 beat 3 is a pose. Game 2 beat 9 is a yelled phrase.

## 2. Requirements

**REQ-07-001.** Phase A and Phase B are played in one classroom.

> Merge rule. “Phase B starts immediately in the same room.”

**REQ-07-002.** The teacher’s desk is at the front of the room.

> Game 1. “sleeping at their desk at the front of the room.”

**REQ-07-003.** In Phase A the teacher is sleeping at that desk.

> Game 1. “The teacher is sleeping at their desk at the front of the room.”

**REQ-07-004.** While sleeping at the desk, the teacher is guarding the target.

> Game 1. “guarding the target (a golden trophy, a toy, or a bucket of treats).”

**REQ-07-005.** The guarded target is a golden trophy, a toy, or a bucket of treats.

> Game 1. “a golden trophy, a toy, or a bucket of treats.”

**REQ-07-006.** A kid claims that target prize by reaching the desk without getting caught.

> Game 1 beat 5. “When a kid successfully reaches the desk without getting caught, they get to … claim the target prize.” Game 1 also names the same target as what the kids “grab.”

**REQ-07-007.** Phase A starts with the kids at the back of the room.

> Game 1. “The kids must sneak up from the back of the room.”

**REQ-07-008.** On the squeaky-shoe walk, the kids hold a squeaky dog toy or bubble wrap under their feet.

> Game 1 beat 1. “holding a squeaky dog toy or bubble wrap under their feet.”

**REQ-07-009.** A kid throws a soft plush toy onto the designated shield mat.

> Game 1 beat 2. “throw a soft plush toy onto a designated shield mat.”

**REQ-07-010.** The room has a mid-way checkpoint line.

> Game 1 beat 4. “To pass a mid-way checkpoint line.”

**REQ-07-011.** A kid passes that line by sticking a funny sticky note on the teacher’s back without the teacher noticing.

> Game 1 beat 4. “stick a funny sticky note … onto the teacher’s back without the teacher noticing.”

**REQ-07-012.** The text samples that funny sticky note with a drawing of a mustache or a goofy face.

> Game 1 beat 4. “like a drawing of a mustache or a goofy face.”

**REQ-07-013.** The kid who claims the target pops a safe party popper or throws a handful of paper confetti over the teacher’s head.

> Game 1 beat 5. “pop a safe party popper or throw a handful of paper confetti over the teacher’s head.”

**REQ-07-014.** Props already in the room for Phase A are still in the room for Phase B, and the desk is still the desk at the front.

> Merge rule. “Props and the room carry from Phase A into Phase B.”

**REQ-07-015.** In Phase B the kids are at the back of the classroom.

> Game 2. “The kids are at the back of the classroom.”

**REQ-07-016.** Phase B has a whiteboard. The teacher faces that whiteboard in the facing pretense, and beat 8’s board is that whiteboard.

> Game 2. “standing facing a whiteboard.” Game 2 beat 8. “turns around to face the board.”

**REQ-07-017.** When the strict teacher is typing, the teacher is typing on a computer. When the strict teacher is reading, the teacher is reading a big book.

> Game 2. “typing on a computer, or reading a big book.”

**REQ-07-018.** The kids deliver a funny secret note to the target mailbox.

> Game 2. “deliver a funny secret note to a target mailbox.”

**REQ-07-019.** The target mailbox is on the other side of the room.

> Game 2. “a target mailbox on the other side of the room.”

**REQ-07-020.** A delivered secret note is in that mailbox.

> Game 2 beat 10. “Once the secret note reaches the target mailbox.”

**REQ-07-021.** The floor is lined with cushions, and the kids step on those cushions.

> Game 2 beat 6. “The floor is lined with cushions.” “which ones are safe to step on.”

**REQ-07-022.** Three of the cushions have hidden whoopee cushions underneath. A wrong step is a step on one of those three, and it makes a loud, funny noise.

> Game 2 beat 6. “three of them have hidden whoopee cushions underneath.” “loud, funny noises if they pick wrong.”

**REQ-07-023.** The kids pass a giant, oversized pair of clown sunglasses down the line using only their feet, without dropping them.

> Game 2 beat 7. “pass a giant, oversized pair of clown sunglasses down the line using only their feet, without dropping them.”

**REQ-07-024.** The kids draw a funny hat or giant ears on the teacher’s shadow on the wall, using a flashlight, when the teacher faces the board.

> Game 2 beat 8. “draw a funny hat or giant ears on the teacher’s shadow on the wall using a flashlight.”

**REQ-07-025.** Once the secret note is in the mailbox, the kids gently spray silly string on the teacher’s desk or on the teacher’s shoes. The sprayed desk is the front desk from Phase A.

> Game 2 beat 10. “gently spray the teacher’s desk (or the teacher’s shoes) with silly string.” Merge rule. The desk carries into Phase B.

## 4. Unknowns

- Where the designated shield mat sits in the room, and who designates it.
- Any finer place for the checkpoint line than “mid-way,” and what the line is made of.
- Which wall holds the teacher’s shadow, and what casts that shadow.
- Any finer place for the mailbox than “the other side,” including whether that side is the front.
- Where the computer sits, and where the big book sits.
- Whether the guarded target rests on the desktop or only with the teacher at the desk. After a kid claims it, where it sits in Phase B.
- Where the secret note starts, and which kid holds it before it reaches the mailbox.
- Where the sunglasses are before the foot pass, and where the flashlight is before the drawing.
- How many cushions line the floor, which three hide the whoopee cushions, and whether each of those three hides one whoopee cushion.
- How many kids, and therefore how many under-foot toys, plush toys, sticky notes, poppers, confetti handfuls, and flashlights.
- Whether a kid may use the party popper and the paper confetti on the same claim, and whether both objects are in the room together. Each is a named blast prop.
- Whether the three target kinds can sit out together while one of them is the guarded target.
- Whether every kid on beat 1 uses the same under-foot item.
- Whether the kids spray both the desk and the shoes, or one of them.
- Whether one drawing is a hat, giant ears, or both.
- Whether Game 2’s props are already in the room during Phase A. They are in the room for Phase B. Phase A’s props stay.
- Whether a thrown plush stays on the mat, and whether the sticky note stays on the teacher’s back after the teacher stands up. Both props remain in the room (REQ-07-014). Their Phase B spot is not restated.
- Where the popper or the confetti ends after it goes over the teacher’s head.
- No beat says a prop is rebuilt, replaced, or cleared. Prop reset is UNKNOWN for every beat.
- What “safe” adds to the party popper, and what “gently” requires of the silly string, beyond those words.

## 5. Handoff lines

- Session loop: Phase A ends on the claim with the victory blast; Phase B ends on the silly-string finale; the session is won only after both.
- Phases, setup, and reset: when each prop is issued; this slice finds no written prop rebuild.
- Teacher behavior: sleeping, guarding, waking, the laugh from the plush, standing up strict, facing the board, typing, reading, and turning toward the doodle or the yell.
- Movement and failure: the sneak, the freeze, three steps back, the cushion guess, the feet-only pass, the race to the shadow, and the crawl. Beat 7’s line is the line of kids passing the sunglasses.
- Sleeping-teacher beats: beats 1–5 beyond the prop each beat uses.
- Escape beats: beats 6–10 beyond the prop each beat uses, including the victory dance.
- Options and replay: which named “or” is in play, the sticky-note samples, and replay.
- Setup and safety: the safe party popper, the soft plush, and the gentle silly-string spray.
- Coverage audit: 22 named props; the guarded target and the secret note are the two prizes; beats 3 and 9 add none.
