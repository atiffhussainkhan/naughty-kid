# Slice 05 — Phase A beats

Player-facing requirements for Phase A of the combined classroom game. Source of truth: Game 1 beats 1–5, the Game 1 opening those beats sit in, and the merge rule where it orders or ends this phase. No new props, no new jokes, no invented send-backs.

The laugh in beat 2 lasts **5 seconds**. The popper in beat 5 stays a **safe party popper**.

## 1. Scope

In scope:

- The five Phase A beats, in the written order: squeaky toy shoes, feather tickle trigger, copycat pose, sticky note sabotage, confetti victory blast.
- Every object, action, timing, and success test those beats state.
- The Game 1 opening that names the sleeping teacher, the desk, the target prize, the sneak from the back, and the wake-up freeze.
- What this phase sets up, which stated options a beat allows, and the one stated reset (three steps back).
- The merge-rule end of Phase A, and the carry of props and the room out of Phase A.

Out of scope, except as a handoff line:

- Phase B, beats 6–10, the teacher standing up, and the strict teacher.
- The session win that waits for both finales.
- Any failure rule a beat does not itself state.

No MERGE-CHOICE is recorded in this slice. The Phase A sentences can be followed together. Gaps stay in Unknowns.

## 2. Requirements

### Phase A frame

**REQ-05-001.** Phase A is Game 1 and runs The Squeaky Toy Shoes, The Feather Tickle Trigger, The Copycat Pose, The Sticky Note Sabotage, and The Confetti Victory Blast in that order. (Merge rule)

**REQ-05-002.** Phase A ends when a kid claims the prize with the confetti victory blast. (Merge rule)

**REQ-05-003.** The teacher is sleeping at their desk. (Game 1)

**REQ-05-004.** The teacher’s desk is at the front of the room. (Game 1)

**REQ-05-005.** The sleeping teacher is guarding the target prize. (Game 1)

**REQ-05-006.** The target prize is a golden trophy, a toy, or a bucket of treats. (Game 1)

**REQ-05-007.** The kids sneak up from the back of the room to grab the target prize. (Game 1)

**REQ-05-008.** If the teacher wakes up, the kids immediately freeze in a ridiculous, funny pose. (Game 1)

### Beat 1 — The Squeaky Toy Shoes

Source sentence: kids must walk across the room while holding a squeaky dog toy or bubble wrap under their feet; sneaking silently is impossible and sounds hilarious.

**REQ-05-009.** In The Squeaky Toy Shoes, the kids walk across the room. (Game 1 beat 1)

**REQ-05-010.** While they walk, the kids hold a squeaky dog toy or bubble wrap under their feet. (Game 1 beat 1)

**REQ-05-011.** Sneaking silently through The Squeaky Toy Shoes is impossible. (Game 1 beat 1)

**REQ-05-012.** Walking with the squeaky dog toy or bubble wrap under their feet sounds hilarious. (Game 1 beat 1)

**REQ-05-013.** Squeaky Toy Shoes success test: the kids have walked across the room while holding a squeaky dog toy or bubble wrap under their feet, and that walk was not silent. (Game 1 beat 1)

### Beat 2 — The Feather Tickle Trigger

Source sentence: if the teacher starts to wake up, a kid can throw a soft plush toy onto a designated shield mat to instantly force the teacher to start laughing uncontrollably for 5 seconds, giving everyone time to move. The beat name is The Feather Tickle Trigger. The thrown object the sentence names is a soft plush toy.

**REQ-05-014.** If the teacher starts to wake up, a kid can throw a soft plush toy onto the designated shield mat. (Game 1 beat 2)

**REQ-05-015.** A soft plush toy thrown onto the designated shield mat instantly forces the teacher to start laughing uncontrollably. (Game 1 beat 2)

**REQ-05-016.** That uncontrollable laugh lasts 5 seconds. (Game 1 beat 2)

**REQ-05-017.** Those 5 seconds give everyone time to move. (Game 1 beat 2)

**REQ-05-018.** Feather Tickle Trigger success test: the soft plush toy is on the designated shield mat, the teacher started laughing uncontrollably at that instant, the laugh lasts 5 seconds, and everyone has time to move during those 5 seconds. (Game 1 beat 2)

### Beat 3 — The Copycat Pose

Source sentence: when the teacher suddenly opens their eyes, anyone caught moving must instantly freeze and imitate a goofy statue (like balancing on one foot with a finger on their nose); if they laugh, they have to take three steps back.

**REQ-05-019.** When the teacher suddenly opens their eyes, anyone caught moving must instantly freeze. (Game 1 beat 3)

**REQ-05-020.** Anyone caught moving must imitate a goofy statue. (Game 1 beat 3)

**REQ-05-021.** The stated example of a goofy statue is balancing on one foot with a finger on their nose. (Game 1 beat 3)

**REQ-05-022.** If a kid who was caught moving laughs, that kid takes three steps back. (Game 1 beat 3)

**REQ-05-023.** Copycat Pose success test: a kid who was caught moving has instantly frozen and is imitating a goofy statue. (Game 1 beat 3)

**REQ-05-024.** Copycat Pose send-back test: a kid who was caught moving and laughs has taken three steps back. (Game 1 beat 3)

### Beat 4 — The Sticky Note Sabotage

Source sentence: to pass a mid-way checkpoint line, a kid must successfully sneak up and stick a funny sticky note (like a drawing of a mustache or a goofy face) onto the teacher’s back without the teacher noticing.

**REQ-05-025.** The room has a mid-way checkpoint line. (Game 1 beat 4)

**REQ-05-026.** Passing the mid-way checkpoint line requires a kid to sneak up to the teacher. (Game 1 beat 4)

**REQ-05-027.** Passing the mid-way checkpoint line requires that kid to stick a funny sticky note onto the teacher’s back. (Game 1 beat 4)

**REQ-05-028.** Passing the mid-way checkpoint line requires that the teacher does not notice the sticky note. (Game 1 beat 4)

**REQ-05-029.** Stated examples of the funny sticky note are a drawing of a mustache and a drawing of a goofy face. (Game 1 beat 4)

**REQ-05-030.** Sticky Note Sabotage success test: a funny sticky note is on the teacher’s back and the teacher has not noticed it. (Game 1 beat 4)

**REQ-05-031.** The mid-way checkpoint line stays unpassed until the Sticky Note Sabotage success test is met. (Game 1 beat 4)

### Beat 5 — The Confetti Victory Blast

Source sentence: when a kid successfully reaches the desk without getting caught, they get to pop a safe party popper or throw a handful of paper confetti over the teacher’s head as they claim the target prize.

**REQ-05-032.** When a kid successfully reaches the desk without getting caught, that kid gets the confetti victory blast. (Game 1 beat 5)

**REQ-05-033.** One confetti-victory-blast action is to pop a safe party popper. (Game 1 beat 5)

**REQ-05-034.** The other confetti-victory-blast action is to throw a handful of paper confetti over the teacher’s head. (Game 1 beat 5)

**REQ-05-035.** The kid does that blast as they claim the target prize. (Game 1 beat 5)

**REQ-05-036.** Confetti Victory Blast success test: a kid has reached the desk without getting caught, has claimed the target prize, and, as they claimed it, has either popped a safe party popper or thrown a handful of paper confetti over the teacher’s head. (Game 1 beat 5)

### Carry out of Phase A

**REQ-05-037.** Props from Phase A and the room carry into Phase B. (Merge rule)

## 3. Stages

Phase A is the stage. Its beats are the inner stages, in the order in REQ-05-001. Build means what is set up. Rebuild means what can be reset and tried again. Options are only the choices the source writes with “or” or “like”.

### Phase A

- **Set up:** a sleeping teacher at a desk at the front of the room, guarding one target prize; kids at the back of the room; beats 1–5 in written order.
- **Options:** the target prize is a golden trophy, a toy, or a bucket of treats.
- **Rebuild:** no phase-wide reset is stated. The only stated reset inside Phase A is beat 3’s three steps back.
- **End:** a kid claims the prize with the confetti victory blast. Props and the room then carry forward.

### Beat 1 — The Squeaky Toy Shoes

- **Set up:** a squeaky dog toy or bubble wrap that the kids can hold under their feet while they walk across the room.
- **Options:** squeaky dog toy, or bubble wrap.
- **Rebuild:** none stated.

### Beat 2 — The Feather Tickle Trigger

- **Set up:** a soft plush toy and a designated shield mat. The throw is on offer if the teacher starts to wake up.
- **Options:** a kid can throw. The source does not say the kid must throw.
- **Timing:** the laugh starts instantly and lasts 5 seconds. During those 5 seconds, everyone has time to move.
- **Rebuild:** none stated. The 5-second laugh ending is the stated duration, not a retry rule.

### Beat 3 — The Copycat Pose

- **Set up:** no new prop. The trigger is the teacher suddenly opening their eyes.
- **Options:** imitate a goofy statue. The stated example is balancing on one foot with a finger on their nose.
- **Rebuild:** a kid who was caught moving and then laughs takes three steps back.

### Beat 4 — The Sticky Note Sabotage

- **Set up:** a mid-way checkpoint line and a funny sticky note that can be stuck on the teacher’s back.
- **Options:** stated examples are a drawing of a mustache, or a drawing of a goofy face.
- **Rebuild:** none stated.

### Beat 5 — The Confetti Victory Blast

- **Set up:** a safe party popper, or a handful of paper confetti, at the claim of the target prize when a kid reaches the desk without getting caught.
- **Options:** pop a safe party popper, or throw a handful of paper confetti over the teacher’s head.
- **Rebuild:** none stated.

## 4. Unknowns

UNK-05-01. Beats 1, 2, 4, and 5 state no failure rule and no send-back. None is invented here.

UNK-05-02. “If the teacher wakes up”, “if the teacher starts to wake up”, and “when the teacher suddenly opens their eyes” are three different phrases. Whether they are one moment or a sequence is unstated.

UNK-05-03. The opening freeze is a ridiculous, funny pose. Beat 3’s freeze is a goofy statue. Whether those are the same obligation is unstated.

UNK-05-04. Whether a session must make the teacher start to wake, and must make the teacher open their eyes, so beats 2 and 3 are performed, is unstated. Both beats are written as conditions. They stay in the phase as those conditions.

UNK-05-05. Whether the 5-second laugh can overlap the teacher suddenly opening their eyes is unstated. Both rules stay as written.

UNK-05-06. The beat is named The Feather Tickle Trigger. The thrown object is a soft plush toy. Whether a feather is another object is unstated.

UNK-05-07. What happens if the soft plush toy is thrown and misses the designated shield mat is unstated.

UNK-05-08. Who designates the shield mat, and where it is in the room, are unstated.

UNK-05-09. “Everyone” is given time to move for those 5 seconds. Whether “everyone” includes the teacher is unstated.

UNK-05-10. What “caught moving” looks like, and what happens if a caught kid does not freeze and does not laugh, are unstated.

UNK-05-11. After the three steps back, whether that kid freezes again, keeps moving, or retries the statue is unstated. How long a step is, and back from which spot, are unstated.

UNK-05-12. Whether a kid who was not caught moving and then laughs takes three steps back is unstated. The laugh rule is written for the kids who were caught moving.

UNK-05-13. Whether the example statue is the only statue that counts, and whether other goofy statues count, are unstated.

UNK-05-14. What “mid-way” measures (room, path, or beats) is unstated.

UNK-05-15. Whether one kid’s sticky note lets every kid pass the checkpoint line, or only the kid who placed it, is unstated.

UNK-05-16. What the teacher noticing looks like, and what happens when the teacher notices, are unstated. No send-back is stated for a noticed note.

UNK-05-17. Whether funny sticky notes other than a mustache drawing or a goofy-face drawing count is unstated. Those two are examples.

UNK-05-18. What “without getting caught” means in beat 5, and whether it is the same event as “caught moving” in beat 3, are unstated. No send-back is stated for being caught on the way to the desk.

UNK-05-19. Whether the safe party popper is also aimed over the teacher’s head is unstated. “Over the teacher’s head” is written on the paper-confetti throw.

UNK-05-20. How many sheets are in “a handful” of paper confetti is unstated.

UNK-05-21. Which of the three target prizes is used in a session, and who chooses it, are unstated. The prize “a toy” is not identified with the squeaky dog toy or the soft plush toy.

UNK-05-22. How many kids are required, whether every kid must do the squeaky-shoe walk, and what the other kids do when one kid claims the prize, are unstated. Phase A still ends on that one claim.

UNK-05-23. Whether both the squeaky dog toy and the bubble wrap are present, or the group holds one of them, is unstated. Either object meets the walk.

UNK-05-24. Whether beats 1–5 are separate gates that each finish before the next starts, or rules on one crossing in that order, is unstated. The written order still holds. Beat 4 is the gate for the checkpoint line. Beat 5 is the gate for the claim.

UNK-05-25. No pass test is stated for “sounds hilarious” beyond the walk with the toy or bubble wrap under the feet being audible rather than silent.
