# Slice 10 — Coverage audit

Every sentence of the merge rule and of both games is quoted below and given one player-facing requirement. Beat names and the two game titles are labels, not sentences. Each beat name is carried in that beat’s requirement. This slice does not replace the other slices and does not add a failure rule, a joke, a count, or a measurement.

38 source sentences map to REQ-10-001 through REQ-10-038. REQ-10-039 is the one MERGE-CHOICE those rules require. Requirement count: 39.

Opening paragraphs have no beat number. They are cited as Game 1 or Game 2. Numbered beats are cited as Game 1 beat N or Game 2 beat N. Session rules are cited as Merge rule.

## 1. Scope

One session, two phases, ten beats, in the order written. Phase A is Game 1, Don’t Wake the Sleeping Teacher, beats 1–5, and ends with the confetti victory blast. Phase B is Game 2, Prank the Strict Teacher, beats 6–10, in the same room, and ends with the silly string finale. The session is won only when both finales have happened. Props and the room carry forward. A stage is a phase or a beat. Build is setup. Rebuild is a reset that can be tried again. This is not a city builder.

## 4. Contradictions

1. **Who freezes, and in what pose.** Game 1 says that if the teacher wakes up, the kids must immediately freeze in a ridiculous, funny pose (REQ-10-022). Game 1 beat 3 says that when the teacher suddenly opens their eyes, anyone caught moving must instantly freeze and imitate a goofy statue (REQ-10-026). One line obligates the kids. The other obligates anyone caught moving. One line names a ridiculous, funny pose. The other names a goofy statue. The texts do not say whether “wakes up” and “opens their eyes” are the same moment.

2. **Move, or freeze, while the teacher is waking.** Game 1 beat 2 says the 5-second laugh gives everyone time to move (REQ-10-025). Game 1 says the kids must freeze if the teacher wakes up (REQ-10-022). The texts do not say the laugh suspends the freeze, and they do not say “starts to wake up” is a different moment from “wakes up”.

3. **Teacher already facing the board, and the turn that starts the doodle.** Game 2 allows the teacher to be standing facing a whiteboard (REQ-10-030). Game 2 beat 8 starts when the teacher turns around to face the board (REQ-10-035). The text never tells the teacher to turn away first, so that opening option and that trigger do not meet.

4. **When the kids have won.** Game 2 beat 10 says the kids win the game once the secret note reaches the target mailbox (REQ-10-038). The merge rule says the session is won only when both finales have happened (REQ-10-009). Beat 10 does not mention the confetti victory blast. The merge rule does not say the mailbox line is only a phase win.

5. **Sleeping at the desk, and standing as the strict teacher.** Game 1 places a sleeping teacher at the desk (REQ-10-020). Game 2 places a strict teacher who is facing a board, typing, or reading (REQ-10-030). The merge rule already joins those portraits: Phase B starts with the teacher standing up and becoming the strict teacher (REQ-10-006). A later document keeps that join.

6. **Kid at the desk, and kids at the back, with no gap between phases.** Game 1 beat 5 puts a kid at the desk (REQ-10-029). Game 2 puts the kids at the back (REQ-10-031). Phase B starts immediately in the same room (REQ-10-005). REQ-10-039 is the joining placement. The texts still do not say how the kids move.

The prize in the merge rule and the target in Game 1 are the same claim: beat 5 calls it the target prize at the confetti victory blast. That is one prize, not two.

The beat title Confetti Victory Blast is the finale the merge rule names. Beat 5 still allows a safe party popper or paper confetti. A popper-only claim satisfies the beat sentence. The popper clause does not say confetti is also thrown.

## 5. Facts a later mega document must not invent

**Player count.** The source never says how many kids play. It says kids, a kid, one kid (the monster-voice role in beat 9), anyone, everyone, and the other kids. Do not add a class size, a roster size, a minimum, or a maximum.

**Ages.** No age, grade, or school year is stated. “Kids”, “teacher”, and “homework” do not supply one. Do not add one.

**Room size.** No length, width, height, area, or capacity is stated. “Across the room”, “mid-way”, “the other side”, “front”, and “back” are not measurements. “Three steps back” is a step count in Game 1 beat 3, not a distance in meters. Do not convert any of these into a size.

**Timers other than the 5-second laugh.** The only duration in either game is the 5 seconds of uncontrollable laughter in Game 1 beat 2 (REQ-10-025). “Immediately”, “instantly”, “quickly”, and “starts immediately” are not amounts of time. Do not add a freeze timer, a race timer, a crawl timer, a dance timer, a phase timer, or a session clock.

**Scoring.** No points, lives, stars, ranks, health, or partial credit appear. Session win is both finales (REQ-10-009). “Win the game” in Game 2 beat 10 is not a score. Do not add one.
