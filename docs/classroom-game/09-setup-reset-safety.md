# Slice 09 — Setup, reset, and written safety limits

## 1. Scope

This slice states what is in place before Phase A, what changes before Phase B, what “rebuild” means after a failed beat, and the safety limits written in the source.

“Build” means what is set up for a phase or beat. “Rebuild” means what is reset so that beat can be tried again. The only rebuild the source defines is the send-back in that beat’s own rule.

Safety limits in this slice are only the words the source uses:

- a **safe** party popper
- **paper** confetti
- a **soft** plush
- **gentle** silly string on the teacher’s desk or the teacher’s shoes

No other safety limit is added. What those four words require beyond the words themselves is UNKNOWN.

A setup step the source never states is UNKNOWN. A joining rule is labeled MERGE-CHOICE and is only the smallest rule that lets Phase A and Phase B both stay intact.

## 2. Requirements

REQ-09-001: Before Phase A begins, the teacher is sleeping at the desk at the front of the room. (Game 1)

REQ-09-002: Before Phase A begins, the sleeping teacher is guarding one target prize, and that prize is a golden trophy, a toy, or a bucket of treats. (Game 1)

REQ-09-009: The confetti used in the beat 5 blast is paper confetti, and the throw is a handful. (Game 1 beat 5)

REQ-09-010: The toy thrown onto the shield mat is a soft plush toy. (Game 1 beat 2)

REQ-09-011: Phase B starts immediately in the same room when a kid claims the prize with the beat 5 victory blast. (Merge rule)

REQ-09-012: Before Phase B beats begin, the teacher stands up and becomes the strict teacher. (Merge rule)

REQ-09-013: Props and the room carry from Phase A into Phase B. (Merge rule)

REQ-09-014: Before Phase B play, the teacher is pretending to be very strict and is facing a whiteboard, typing on a computer, or reading a big book. (Game 2)

REQ-09-015: Before Phase B play, a funny secret note is in play and a target mailbox is on the other side of the room. (Game 2)

REQ-09-016: MERGE-CHOICE: Before beat 6 begins, the kids are at the back of the classroom, so Game 2 can start after Phase A ends at the desk. (Merge rule, Game 2)

REQ-09-017: Before beat 6 begins, the floor is lined with cushions, and three of those cushions have hidden whoopee cushions underneath. (Game 2 beat 6)

REQ-09-018: Before beat 7 begins, a giant oversized pair of clown sunglasses is available for the kids to pass. (Game 2 beat 7)

REQ-09-019: Before beat 8 begins, the room has a board the teacher can face, the teacher’s shadow is on the wall, and a flashlight is available. (Game 2 beat 8)

REQ-09-020: The beat 10 finale is a gentle spray of silly string on the teacher’s desk or the teacher’s shoes. (Game 2 beat 10)

REQ-09-021: After a failed beat, the kids are sent back only as that beat’s own rule says. (Merge rule)

REQ-09-022: If a kid laughs while holding the beat 3 statue, that kid takes three steps back, and those three steps are the rebuild for that failure. (Game 1 beat 3, Merge rule)

## 5. Handoff lines

- Session loop: the session is won only after both finales; Phase B starts immediately after the beat 5 claim.
- Phases: beats 1–5 then beats 6–10, with no beat dropped.
- Teacher behavior: sleep, wake, the five-second laugh, standing up, the strict activity, facing the board, and investigating the monster line.
- Movement and failure: the freeze, the statue, the crawl, the feet-only pass, and the wrong-cushion noise. This slice states only the three-step rebuild.
- Sleeping-teacher beats: how beats 1–5 are played once the Phase A setup is in place.
- Strict-teacher beats: how beats 6–10 are played once the Phase B setup is in place.
- Props and space: every named prop and where it sits. Positions not written here stay UNKNOWN.
- Options and replay: the “or” lists in the stages table are choices; the “like” lines are examples. Replay past a beat’s own rule is UNKNOWN.
- Coverage audit: check this slice against setup, carry into Phase B, failure send-back, and the four safety phrases only.
