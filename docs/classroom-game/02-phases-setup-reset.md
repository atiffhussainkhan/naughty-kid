# Slice 02 — Phases, beats, setup, and reset

Player-facing requirements for one classroom session made from Game 1 and Game 2. Stages are Phase A, Phase B, and the ten beats. Set up means what is put in place for that stage. Reset means what that stage says can be undone and tried again. Where a stage states no reset, this file says UNKNOWN.

## 1. Scope

In scope:

- Phase A, then beats 1–5, then Phase B, then beats 6–10, in that order.
- For each of those twelve stages: what is set up, what the players do, what can be reset and tried again, and which alternatives the source text offers.
- Carrying the room and the Phase A props into Phase B.
- The two finales as the ends of the two phases, and the session win that needs both.

Out of scope except as a handoff line: judging details the beats do not state, the words on the secret note, the steps of the victory dance, and who is allowed to pick an alternative.

## 3. Stages

Play order is the order below. There are twelve stages.

### 1. Phase A

- **Set up:** The teacher sleeps at the desk at the front. One target sits with the teacher. The kids are at the back. This room is the room Phase B will use.
- **Do:** The kids sneak from the back to grab the target. If the teacher wakes, they immediately freeze in a ridiculous, funny pose. Beats 1–5 run in order. The phase ends when a kid claims the prize with the confetti victory blast.
- **Reset and try again:** UNKNOWN.
- **Options:** Golden trophy, a toy, or a bucket of treats.
- **Requirements:** REQ-02-001, REQ-02-002, REQ-02-004, REQ-02-005, REQ-02-007, REQ-02-008, REQ-02-009, REQ-02-010, REQ-02-011.

### 2. Beat 1 — The Squeaky Toy Shoes

- **Set up:** A squeaky dog toy or bubble wrap, held under the feet.
- **Do:** The kids walk across the room on that item. Silent sneaking is impossible.
- **Reset and try again:** UNKNOWN.
- **Options:** Squeaky dog toy, or bubble wrap.
- **Requirements:** REQ-02-012, REQ-02-013, REQ-02-014.

### 3. Beat 2 — The Feather Tickle Trigger

- **Set up:** A soft plush toy and a designated shield mat.
- **Do:** If the teacher starts to wake up, a kid can throw the plush onto the shield mat. The teacher then laughs uncontrollably for 5 seconds, and everyone has that time to move.
- **Reset and try again:** UNKNOWN.
- **Options:** The source names no alternate prop. The items are the soft plush toy and the designated shield mat.
- **Requirements:** REQ-02-015, REQ-02-016, REQ-02-017.

### 4. Beat 3 — The Copycat Pose

- **Set up:** No added prop is named.
- **Do:** When the teacher suddenly opens their eyes, anyone caught moving instantly freezes and imitates a goofy statue.
- **Reset and try again:** If a kid laughs, that kid takes three steps back.
- **Options:** The source names one statue: balancing on one foot with a finger on the nose. It gives no closed list of further statues.
- **Requirements:** REQ-02-018, REQ-02-019, REQ-02-020.

### 5. Beat 4 — The Sticky Note Sabotage

- **Set up:** A mid-way checkpoint line and a funny sticky note.
- **Do:** To pass the line, a kid sneaks up and sticks the note on the teacher’s back without the teacher noticing.
- **Reset and try again:** UNKNOWN.
- **Options:** Drawings the source names are a mustache and a goofy face. It gives no closed list of further drawings.
- **Requirements:** REQ-02-021, REQ-02-022, REQ-02-023.

### 6. Beat 5 — The Confetti Victory Blast

- **Set up:** A safe party popper or a handful of paper confetti, at the desk, with the target from Phase A.
- **Do:** A kid who reaches the desk without getting caught claims the target and pops the party popper or throws the paper confetti over the teacher’s head.
- **Reset and try again:** UNKNOWN.
- **Options:** Safe party popper, or a handful of paper confetti. The prize is the Phase A target (golden trophy, toy, or bucket of treats).
- **Requirements:** REQ-02-024, REQ-02-025, REQ-02-026.

### 7. Phase B

- **Set up:** Same room and Phase A props. The teacher stands up and becomes the strict teacher. The kids are at the back (REQ-02-030). A funny secret note and a target mailbox on the other side of the room are in play. The teacher’s activity uses a whiteboard, a computer, or a big book.
- **Do:** Beats 6–10 run in order. The kids deliver the funny secret note to the mailbox. The phase ends with the silly string finale. The session is won only when this finale and the Phase A finale have both happened.
- **Reset and try again:** UNKNOWN.
- **Options:** The strict teacher is standing facing a whiteboard, typing on a computer, or reading a big book.
- **Requirements:** REQ-02-001, REQ-02-003, REQ-02-004, REQ-02-005, REQ-02-027, REQ-02-028, REQ-02-029, REQ-02-030, REQ-02-031.

### 8. Beat 6 — The Whoopee Cushion Pathway

- **Set up:** The floor is lined with cushions. Three of them hide whoopee cushions.
- **Do:** The kids guess which cushions are safe to step on.
- **Reset and try again:** UNKNOWN. A wrong step makes a loud, funny noise. The beat states no send-back and no second try.
- **Options:** The source names no alternate prop.
- **Requirements:** REQ-02-032, REQ-02-033, REQ-02-034.

### 9. Beat 7 — The Reverse Homework Trick

- **Set up:** A giant, oversized pair of clown sunglasses, and the line they are passed down.
- **Do:** Instead of doing work, the kids pass the sunglasses down the line using only their feet, without dropping them.
- **Reset and try again:** UNKNOWN.
- **Options:** The source names no alternate prop.
- **Requirements:** REQ-02-035, REQ-02-036, REQ-02-037.

### 10. Beat 8 — The Doodle Attack

- **Set up:** A flashlight, the teacher’s shadow on the wall, and the board the teacher can face.
- **Do:** When the teacher turns around to face the board, the kids race to draw on the shadow with the flashlight.
- **Reset and try again:** UNKNOWN.
- **Options:** A funny hat, or giant ears.
- **Requirements:** REQ-02-038, REQ-02-039.

### 11. Beat 9 — The Monster Talk Distraction

- **Set up:** No added prop is named.
- **Do:** To move forward, one kid yells a silly phrase in a deep, goofy monster voice. While the teacher turns around, confused, to investigate, the other kids can crawl forward.
- **Reset and try again:** UNKNOWN.
- **Options:** The source names one phrase: “The broccoli has landed!” It gives no closed list of further phrases.
- **Requirements:** REQ-02-040, REQ-02-041, REQ-02-042.

### 12. Beat 10 — The Silly String Finale

- **Set up:** Silly string, the teacher’s desk (still in the room from Phase A), and the teacher’s shoes. The target mailbox from Phase B is the gate.
- **Do:** Once the secret note reaches the mailbox, the kids gently spray the desk or the shoes with silly string while doing a victory dance. This wins Game 2 and ends Phase B.
- **Reset and try again:** UNKNOWN.
- **Options:** The teacher’s desk, or the teacher’s shoes.
- **Requirements:** REQ-02-043, REQ-02-044.

Beat 3 is the only stage whose source text states a reset. The other eleven stages have no reset rule.

## 4. Unknowns

- Who chooses the trophy, the toy, or the bucket of treats; the squeaky dog toy or the bubble wrap; the party popper or the paper confetti; the hat or the giant ears; the desk or the shoes; and which of the three teacher activities is used.
- How many kids play. What “down the line” is made of. How far the beat 1 walk goes before beat 2.
- Whether beat 2 must wait for a wake-up, whether beat 3 must wait for the eyes to open, and whether beat 8 must wait for the teacher to turn, before the next beat starts.
- Whether a missed plush throw can be thrown again. The beat states the successful throw and states no miss.
- Whether a goofy statue other than one foot and a finger on the nose counts. Whether a sticky note other than a mustache or a goofy face counts. Whether a monster phrase other than “The broccoli has landed!” counts.
- Phase A tells every kid to freeze in a ridiculous, funny pose if the teacher wakes. Beat 3 tells anyone caught moving to imitate a goofy statue, and sends a laughing kid three steps back. How those two sentences apply to the same moment is UNKNOWN. What happens when a caught kid does not freeze is UNKNOWN. The length of a step is UNKNOWN.
- What happens when the teacher notices the sticky note, when a kid is caught at the desk, or when the sunglasses are dropped.
- How many cushions are on the floor besides the three whoopee cushions. Whether a loud whoopee step sends anyone back or allows another step. Who hid the whoopee cushions.
- Whether “the board” in beat 8 is the same object as the whiteboard in the teacher-activity list.
- The beat 2 title says “Feather.” The action uses a soft plush toy. No feather prop is stated.
- Whether the Phase A target toy is a different object from the squeaky dog toy and the soft plush toy.
- What the funny secret note says, what the victory dance is, and what “safe” and “gently” require beyond those words.
- Where the claimed prize is held during Phase B.
- Whether a beat, a phase, or the session can be started over. No stage except beat 3 states a retry, and beat 3 states three steps back.

## 5. Handoff lines for other slices

- Who may pick each stated alternative, and how that pick is locked.
- How a freeze, a laugh, being caught, being noticed, a drop, or a wrong cushion is judged, past the words in section 3.
- The secret-note wording, the mailbox, and the victory-dance steps.
- Any send-back a source does not state stays UNKNOWN for the slice that needs it.
