# Slice 03 — Teacher behavior

Player-facing requirements for what the teacher does, what triggers it, and what the kids are allowed to do during it. Sources are Game 1, Game 2, and the merge rule in the classroom-game brief. No new jokes, poses, or penalties.

## 1. Scope

In scope:

- Phase A: sleeping at the desk, starting to wake, the 5-second laugh, eyes opening, waking up, not noticing a sticky note, and getting the victory blast.
- The stand-up that turns the sleeping teacher into the strict teacher when Phase B starts.
- Phase B: pretending to be strict, facing the whiteboard, typing, reading, turning to face the board, turning to investigate a monster voice, and getting silly string.
- What kids may or must do in each of those moments, including the two finales that land on the teacher or the teacher’s desk or shoes.

Out of scope except as a handoff line:

- How the room, checkpoint, mailbox, shield mat, and cushion path are laid out.
- Whoopee-cushion guessing and passing the clown sunglasses, except that the teacher has no stated reaction during those beats.
- Building props, scoring, and any failure penalty a beat does not already write.

## 2. Requirements

### Sleeping

**REQ-03-001.** Phase A starts with the teacher sleeping at the teacher’s desk at the front of the room.

Source: Game 1

**REQ-03-002.** While the teacher is sleeping, the teacher is guarding the target.

Source: Game 1

**REQ-03-003.** The target the sleeping teacher guards is a golden trophy, a toy, or a bucket of treats.

Source: Game 1

**REQ-03-004.** While the teacher is sleeping, the kids must sneak from the back of the room up to the desk to grab the target.

Source: Game 1

**REQ-03-005.** While the teacher is sleeping, kids cross the room holding a squeaky dog toy or bubble wrap under their feet, and sneaking silently is impossible.

Source: Game 1 beat 1

### Starting to wake, then laughing

**REQ-03-006.** If the teacher starts to wake up, a kid may throw a soft plush toy onto the designated shield mat.

Source: Game 1 beat 2

**REQ-03-007.** A soft plush toy thrown onto the designated shield mat instantly forces the teacher to laugh uncontrollably for 5 seconds.

Source: Game 1 beat 2

**REQ-03-008.** During those 5 seconds, every kid may move.

Source: Game 1 beat 2

### Eyes open, and waking up

**REQ-03-009.** When the teacher suddenly opens their eyes, any kid who is caught moving must instantly freeze and imitate a goofy statue.

Source: Game 1 beat 3

**REQ-03-010.** Balancing on one foot with a finger on the nose counts as a goofy statue for that freeze.

Source: Game 1 beat 3

**REQ-03-011.** If a kid laughs during that freeze, that kid must take three steps back.

Source: Game 1 beat 3

**REQ-03-012.** If the teacher wakes up, the kids must immediately freeze in a ridiculous, funny pose.

Source: Game 1

### Not noticing a sticky note

**REQ-03-013.** A kid may sneak up and stick a funny sticky note on the teacher’s back.

Source: Game 1 beat 4

**REQ-03-014.** A drawing of a mustache, and a drawing of a goofy face, each count as a funny sticky note for that stick.

Source: Game 1 beat 4

**REQ-03-015.** The sticky note succeeds only when the teacher does not notice it.

Source: Game 1 beat 4

**REQ-03-016.** A kid whose sticky note the teacher does not notice may pass the mid-way checkpoint line.

Source: Game 1 beat 4

### Getting confetti

**REQ-03-017.** A kid who reaches the desk without getting caught may claim the target.

Source: Game 1 beat 5

**REQ-03-018.** As that kid claims the target, the kid may pop a safe party popper or throw a handful of paper confetti over the teacher’s head.

Source: Game 1 beat 5

**REQ-03-019.** MERGE-CHOICE: Popping the safe party popper and throwing the handful of paper confetti both count as the confetti victory blast, and claiming the prize with either one ends Phase A.

Source: Game 1 beat 5; Merge rule

### Standing up into Phase B

**REQ-03-020.** Phase B starts immediately, in the same room, when Phase A ends.

Source: Merge rule

**REQ-03-021.** At the start of Phase B, the teacher stands up and becomes the strict teacher.

Source: Merge rule

**REQ-03-022.** MERGE-CHOICE: Phase B opens with the kids at the back of the classroom.

Source: Merge rule; Game 2

**REQ-03-023.** When the teacher stands up, the room and the props from Phase A are still there.

Source: Merge rule

### Facing the whiteboard, typing, reading

**REQ-03-024.** The strict teacher pretends to be very strict.

Source: Game 2

**REQ-03-025.** Standing facing a whiteboard is a strict-teacher activity.

Source: Game 2

**REQ-03-026.** Typing on a computer is a strict-teacher activity.

Source: Game 2

**REQ-03-027.** Reading a big book is a strict-teacher activity.

Source: Game 2

**REQ-03-028.** The strict teacher is doing one of those three activities: standing facing a whiteboard, typing on a computer, or reading a big book.

Source: Game 2

**REQ-03-029.** While the teacher is the strict teacher, the kids are delivering a funny secret note to a target mailbox on the other side of the room.

Source: Game 2

**REQ-03-030.** Beats 1 through 5 play in the written order, and the teacher stands up only after Phase A ends on the confetti victory blast.

Source: Merge rule

**REQ-03-031.** After the teacher stands up, beats 6 through 10 play in the written order.

Source: Merge rule

### Turning to the board

**REQ-03-032.** When the teacher turns around to face the board, the kids must quickly race to draw a funny hat or giant ears on the teacher’s shadow on the wall using a flashlight.

Source: Game 2 beat 8

### Turning to investigate a monster voice

**REQ-03-033.** To move forward, one kid must yell a silly phrase in a deep, goofy monster voice.

Source: Game 2 beat 9

**REQ-03-034.** “The broccoli has landed!” counts as a silly phrase for that yell.

Source: Game 2 beat 9

**REQ-03-035.** That yell makes the teacher turn around, confused, to investigate the sound.

Source: Game 2 beat 9

**REQ-03-036.** While the teacher is investigating that sound, the other kids may crawl forward.

Source: Game 2 beat 9

### Getting silly string

**REQ-03-037.** When the secret note reaches the target mailbox, the kids may gently spray the teacher’s desk or the teacher’s shoes with silly string while doing a victory dance.

Source: Game 2 beat 10

**REQ-03-038.** That silly-string spray is the Phase B finale and ends Phase B.

Source: Game 2 beat 10; Merge rule

**REQ-03-039.** The session is won only when both the confetti victory blast and the silly string finale have happened.

Source: Merge rule

### Failed beats

**REQ-03-040.** A failed beat sends the kids back by the rule that beat itself writes.

Source: Merge rule

The only teacher-timed kid setback written in this slice is REQ-03-011. Beats with no failure sentence are UNKNOWN below.

## 3. Stages

Beats run in the written order. Phase A is beats 1–5. Phase B is beats 6–10 and starts when the teacher stands up.

| Stage | Teacher setup | Kids during it | Reset |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Phase A open | Asleep at the front desk, guarding one target | Sneak from the back toward the desk | None written |
| Beat 1 | Still the sleeping teacher | Cross with a squeaky dog toy or bubble wrap underfoot | None written |
| Beat 2 | Starts to wake, if that condition happens | One kid may throw a plush onto the shield mat; then everyone may move for 5 seconds | None written for the teacher after the laugh |
| Beat 3 | Suddenly opens their eyes; the intro also has the teacher wake up | Caught movers freeze as a goofy statue; a kid who laughs takes three steps back; the intro freezes the kids in a ridiculous, funny pose | Three steps back for the kid who laughs |
| Beat 4 | Does not notice a successful sticky note | Sneak a funny note onto the teacher’s back; an unnoticed note passes the mid-way line | None written |
| Beat 5 | Receives the victory blast over the head if confetti is chosen | Claim the target and pop a safe party popper or throw paper confetti | Phase ends on success; no failure sentence |
| Phase B open | Stands up and is the strict teacher | Kids are at the back (MERGE-CHOICE REQ-03-022) | Stand-up is not written as reversible |
| Beats 6–7 | Strict teacher; activity is facing the whiteboard, typing, or reading | Whoopee path, then the sunglasses pass; no teacher reaction is written | None written for the teacher |
| Beat 8 | Turns around to face the board | Race to draw a funny hat or giant ears on the teacher’s shadow | None written |
| Beat 9 | Turns around, confused, to investigate the yell | One kid yells; the other kids may crawl | None written |
| Beat 10 | Desk or shoes get silly string | Gentle spray and a victory dance | Phase ends on success; no failure sentence |

Setup options that exist:

- Guarded target: a golden trophy, a toy, or a bucket of treats.
- Under-foot noisemaker: a squeaky dog toy, or bubble wrap.
- Victory blast: a safe party popper, or a handful of paper confetti over the teacher’s head.
- Strict-teacher activity: standing facing a whiteboard, typing on a computer, or reading a big book.
- Shadow drawing: a funny hat, or giant ears.
- Silly-string target: the teacher’s desk, or the teacher’s shoes.

Named examples that count, without a closed list: the one-foot nose-touch statue, a mustache drawing, a goofy-face drawing, and the yell “The broccoli has landed!”

Rebuild that is written: a kid who laughs during the eyes-open freeze takes three steps back. No sentence resets the teacher to sleep, closes the eyes, stops the laugh early, or sits the strict teacher back down.

## 4. Unknowns

1. UNKNOWN: what makes the teacher start to wake, and what makes the teacher open their eyes. Squeaky shoes, whoopee cushions, and any other noise have no stated effect on the teacher.
2. UNKNOWN: whether “starts to wake,” “wakes up,” and “opens their eyes” are one moment, a sequence, or separate events. What the teacher does when the 5-second laugh ends is unstated, including whether the eyes then open.
3. UNKNOWN: how the intro’s freeze for every kid fits the beat 3 freeze for kids caught moving. How long either freeze lasts is unstated. What a kid who is not caught moving may do when the eyes open is unstated.
4. UNKNOWN: what “caught” means, and which teacher action catches a kid at the desk.
5. UNKNOWN: what the teacher does if the teacher notices the sticky note. Beat 4 has no failure rule.
6. UNKNOWN: whether the teacher leaves the chair during any Phase A wake. Standing up is written only at the Phase B start. The teacher’s reaction to the party popper and to confetti is unstated.
7. UNKNOWN: which strict activity is active, who chooses it, and whether it changes during Phase B. What the teacher is facing just before turning to the board is unstated. Whether typing or reading already faces the board is unstated.
8. UNKNOWN: how long the monster-voice investigation lasts, what the yelling kid may do during it, and which way the teacher faces when it ends.
9. UNKNOWN: the teacher’s reaction to silly string. What the teacher does after the finale is unstated.
10. UNKNOWN: how the kids get to the back of the room for the Phase B opening. REQ-03-022 sets only that they are there.
11. UNKNOWN: whether a sticky note stuck in Phase A is still on the teacher’s back after the stand-up. Props remain in the room; staying stuck is unstated.
12. UNKNOWN: whether any pose, note, or monster phrase besides the named examples counts.
13. UNKNOWN: any teacher reset on a failed beat. Beats 1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, and 10 write no failure rule.

## 5. Handoff lines

- Room: front desk, back of the room, mid-way checkpoint line, whiteboard, shadow on the wall, shield-mat place, mailbox on the other side, cushion path.
- Kid travel with no teacher sentence: whoopee-cushion guesses, passing the clown sunglasses with feet, how far three steps are, and how a crawl is performed.
- Props: plush, shield mat, sticky notes, party popper, confetti, flashlight, silly string, guarded target, secret note, computer, big book.
- Note delivery: moving the secret note until it reaches the mailbox, which is the trigger for the silly-string finale.
- Retry: penalties for a noticed note, a wrong cushion, a dropped pair of sunglasses, or getting caught before the claim. This slice does not add them.
