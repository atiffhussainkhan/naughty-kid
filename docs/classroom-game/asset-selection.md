# Asset selection

Stills come from [atiffhussainkhan/Image](https://github.com/atiffhussainkhan/Image) (public). Motion was checked in [atiffhussainkhan/animation](https://github.com/atiffhussainkhan/animation) (private). Both were cloned with depth 1 under `/tmp/asset-repos`.

The kids, the teacher, the desk, the big book, and the trophy are colorful cartoons from the cleaned Education and cartoon folders in release `game-art-search-v2-2026-10-05`. They share thick outlines and bright colors, so the stage reads as one classroom. The room paint (blue wall, rainbow rail, wood floor, blue-framed whiteboard) is page styling around those drawings.

Licences on this clipart are unverified.
