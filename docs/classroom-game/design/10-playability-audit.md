# Playability audit — slice 10

Audit of the core document only: `docs/classroom-game/mega-requirements.md`. The four decisions in the design brief are used below and marked DESIGN-DECISION. They are not source requirements. This file adds no beat and no prop. Recommendations are the smallest rule that lets one group play one session. A later pass applies requirement fixes. This file does not edit the core document.

## 1. What this slice designs

Whether a host can run the 76 live requirements in one room, in one sitting, through beats 1–10 and both finales. Three lists:

- Duplicates still sitting in the core document (6).
- Requirements a host cannot run from the text alone.
- Holes that would make another design slice guess, each with one recommendation.

Section 1, the prop catalog, the choice table, and the MERGE-CHOICE index repeat section 3 on purpose. The core document says they are a map, not a second rule set. They are not counted as duplicates.

## 2. The design

### Duplicates still in the core document

**D1. REQ-MEGA-012 and REQ-MEGA-050.** Same kids, same moment. Eyes open, anyone caught moving freezes, and that freeze is the statue. Two ids, one host check.

**D2. REQ-MEGA-038, REQ-MEGA-040, and the sentence after REQ-MEGA-077.** One policy, three writings. A send-back happens only when that beat already writes one, and the only written send-back is beat 3’s three steps. REQ-MEGA-014 is the step rule itself, so it is not part of this duplicate.

**D3. REQ-MEGA-039 and REQ-MEGA-071.** One lock, two writings. A beat with no failure rule has no failure result, and a later pass does not add one.

**D4. REQ-MEGA-054, REQ-MEGA-055, and the action list inside REQ-MEGA-019.** Popper and confetti are defined, then named again as the blast. The words that are not a copy are “any one of these is the confetti victory blast.”

**D5. REQ-MEGA-015.** “Both sentences stay” repeats the fact that REQ-MEGA-007 and REQ-MEGA-012 are already in the list. A host performs 007 and 012. 015 gives the host nothing to run.

**D6. REQ-MEGA-047 beside REQ-MEGA-006 and REQ-MEGA-018.** Start at the back, and take the prize, are already those two requirements plus the beat order. 047 says the kids sneak from the back and grab the prize, with no beat number. A host can read it as permission to take the prize during the sneak. REQ-MEGA-041 and REQ-MEGA-073 already keep the grab on beat 5.

Not counted, because each half changes the host’s job:

- REQ-MEGA-022 (“pretends to be very strict”) has no check of its own. It is unrunnable, not a copy of REQ-MEGA-057. See requirement fixes.

### Holes, and the smallest recommendation for each

The three that most threaten a playable session are H1, H2, and H3. A group that improvises the smaller holes can still stall on these three: Phase B never reaches beat 10, the finale’s start never happens, and the room cannot legally change from Phase A to Phase B.

**H1. Threat. Beats 6, 7, and 9 have no finish.** Unknown 7. The host cannot start beat 7, beat 8, beat 9, or beat 10. The silly string finale never arrives. Beats 1 and 2 have the same hole (unknowns 3 and 5) and are included here so the gate order is one rule.

**Recommendation.** The ten beats are gates. The next gate starts when the current gate’s end below is true. One crossing does not play later beats early.

- Beat 1 ends when every kid has walked forward from the back and stopped on the back side of the checkpoint line.
- Beat 2: the host opens the “starts to wake” window once. If the plush hits the mat, the 5-second laugh runs, then the beat ends and the teacher is asleep again. If the plush never hits, the host closes that window and the beat ends with the teacher still asleep.
- Beat 6 ends when the kids have crossed the cushioned floor from the back to the mailbox side. A wrong step makes the noise and the kids keep going.
- Beat 7 ends when the sunglasses have gone from the kid at the back end of the line to the kid at the mailbox end, feet only.
- Beat 9 ends when the host, having turned to investigate the yell, turns back. The secret-note delivery in H2 happens at that end.

**H2. Threat. Nothing moves the secret note.** Unknown 40. REQ-MEGA-026 requires a delivery. REQ-MEGA-034 starts beat 10 only when the note reaches the mailbox. Beats 6–9 never mention the note. The session cannot be won, because REQ-MEGA-036 waits on the silly string finale.

**Recommendation.** Before beat 6, the note is at the back with one kid. That kid carries it on beats 6, 7, 8, and 9. When beat 9 ends, that kid puts it in the mailbox. That placement starts beat 10. The note does not reach the mailbox before beat 9. The words on the note stay unwritten.

**H3. Threat. Phase A ends at the desk and Phase B must open at the back.** REQ-MEGA-023. Unknowns 27 and 49. No path is written. The cushion floor is a Phase B prop and is not placed by any beat. If the host leaves the kids at the desk, beat 6 has no start. If the host lays the cushions during Phase A, the sneak in beats 1–5 has no floor.

**Recommendation.** The blast ends Phase A. The kids then walk to the back of the room. That walk is the join already left open by REQ-MEGA-023. It is not a new beat and it has no number. Before beat 1, the host has placed the mailbox, the secret note, the sunglasses, the flashlight, and the silly string at the sides, off the Phase A path. While the kids walk back, the host lays the cushions and hides the three whoopee cushions. Beat 6 starts after that lay, with the kids at the back. “Immediately” stays a word with no clock.

**H4. The three wake phrases have no cause and no length.** Unknowns 13–16. DESIGN-DECISION 1 separates the moments. DESIGN-DECISION 2 covers movement during the laugh. The host still needs a way to fire each moment.

**DESIGN-DECISION 1.** Three wake moments. “Starts to wake” is only the beat 2 window. “Wakes up” freezes every kid in a still pose. “Opens their eyes” is the beat 3 statue for anyone still moving.

**DESIGN-DECISION 2.** During the 5-second laugh, kids may move. That window is the exception. Outside it, a wake freezes them.

**Recommendation.** The host calls each moment. No noise and no prop causes them. “Starts to wake” is called once, in beat 2 only. “Opens their eyes” is called once, in beat 3 only. “Wakes up” may be called once on beat 1, once on beat 4, and once on beat 5. The host does not call “wakes up” or “opens their eyes” during the 5-second laugh. A “wakes up” pose is any pose the kid holds still. The host does not grade it. It lasts until the host tells the kids to continue the current beat. A kid who is already holding still when eyes open is not caught. A kid who is still moving takes the written statue: one foot, a finger on the nose, until the host says to continue. A kid who misses the freeze is told once to take it. That reminder adds no steps.

**H5. “Caught” on beat 5, and a claim with no blast.** Unknowns 2 and 17. REQ-MEGA-018 and REQ-MEGA-056 can disagree.

**Recommendation.** On beat 5, “caught” means this kid was already put in a “wakes up” pose or an eyes-open statue before the claim. That kid does not claim. Another kid may claim. One uncaught kid, at the desk, claims the prize and does the one chosen blast. That pair of actions ends Phase A for the whole session. Other kids do not have to reach the desk. A reach with no blast does not end Phase A.

**H6. Beat 4 reaches the teacher, and beat 5 owns the desk.** REQ-MEGA-051, REQ-MEGA-052, REQ-MEGA-073. Unknowns 18 and 19.

**Recommendation.** Before the session the host marks the existing checkpoint line by eye, between the back of the room and the desk. It is not measured. On beat 4 one kid goes to the teacher’s back, sticks one note, and leaves the prize where it is. This session the note shows either the mustache or the goofy face. The sleeping teacher does not notice. That one unnoticed note passes the whole group. Noticing is not a branch the host plays.

**H7. Beat 3’s three steps have no direction.** Unknowns 11 and 12. The step count is already written. A length would be a new measurement, so none is added.

**Recommendation.** Only a kid who was caught moving, and who then laughs, takes the three steps. Other kids who laugh stay. “Back” faces the back of the room, the Phase A start. After the three steps, that kid continues beat 3 from the new spot. Each step is that kid’s own step.

**H8. Real choices have no picker.** Unknowns 23, 24, and 26.

**Recommendation.** The host picks before beat 1, announces the pick, and locks it for the session: one prize kind, one under-foot item for every kid, one blast, one strict-teacher activity, one shadow drawing, one silly-string target. The unchosen partner need not be in the room. The under-foot item is under the feet for beat 1 only. One claim uses one blast. Confetti, if chosen, goes over the teacher’s head. A popped popper, if chosen, is popped at the desk.

**H9. Shield mat.** Unknown 20.

**Recommendation.** The host places the mat beside the desk and points to it before beat 1. During the beat 2 window, a miss may be thrown again. The plush stays on the mat through the 5-second laugh. After the laugh the teacher is asleep again (H1).

**H10. Whoopee path.** Unknowns 9, 35, and 46, for this beat.

**Recommendation.** The host hides one whoopee cushion under each of three lining cushions before beat 6 and does not tell the kids. No total cushion count is set. A sounded whoopee stays under its cushion. After the noise, the kids take their next step toward the mailbox side (H1). The noise is not a send-back.

**H11. Sunglasses pass.** Unknowns 10 and 36.

**Recommendation.** All the kids form one line. “Down” runs from the back end toward the mailbox end. The sunglasses start at the feet of the kid at the back end. There is no separate work task. If the pair drops, the kids get it back under a foot at that spot and continue the feet-only pass. No steps back.

**H12. Doodle window length, shadow, and race.** Unknowns 30, 37, and 38. The turn itself is DESIGN-DECISION 3.

**DESIGN-DECISION 3.** “The board” means the whiteboard. If the teacher is already facing it, the doodle window opens when they turn away and then turn back. If they are typing or reading, the doodle window opens when they turn to face the whiteboard.

**Recommendation.** The host keeps the one chosen activity from the Phase B stand-up until this window. The unused computer or book may sit at the front and is not a second activity. The kids move to the teacher’s shadow on the wall the teacher is facing. The flashlight starts at the back with the kids and is only the drawing tool. One kid draws the one chosen shape, hat or ears. The host holds the facing until that drawing is on the shadow, then turns away. That ends beat 8.

**H13. Monster yell.** Unknowns 21 and 39, for this beat. Finish is in H1.

**Recommendation.** This session the yell is “The broccoli has landed!” in a deep monster voice. One kid yells. The host turns away from the kids’ path to investigate, then turns back (H1). “Forward” is toward the mailbox. The other kids may crawl that way while the host is turned. The yelling kid does not have to crawl. The beat still ends if nobody crawled. No other movement is required.

**H14. Spray and dance.** Unknowns 41 and 42. Dance steps are outside the session, so none are written here.

**Recommendation.** After the note is in the mailbox, the kids spray the one chosen target, desk or shoes, with one gentle burst of the silly string already in the prop list. Every kid dances in place while they spray. The host does not play an off-target spray or a spray with no dance. Phase B ends when that spray and dance have happened. DESIGN-DECISION 4: that ending wins Phase B. The session is already waiting on this finale plus the earlier blast. Winning Phase B does not skip Phase A, and Phase A has already been played.

**H15. Props, carry, and cleanup.** Unknowns 33, 34, 44, 46, and 47.

**Recommendation.** The host places every prop before the beat that uses it (H3 for the cushion timing). A finished beat stays finished. The three steps move a kid. They do not undo a passed line, a claim, or a finale. The claimed prize stays with the kid who claimed it. The sticky note may stay on the teacher’s back. Phase A props stay in the room. The host moves one aside only when it covers the cushion path. Nothing is rebuilt, replaced, or cleared.

**H16. Teacher’s body during Phase A, and Phase A rules after the stand-up.** Unknowns 31 and 50.

**Recommendation.** The teacher stays at the desk through Phase A. Standing up is the Phase B start (REQ-MEGA-021). Wake poses, the statue, and the three steps stop when the teacher stands up. Phase B does not use them.

**H17. How many kids.** Unknown 1. A number is forbidden by the core document.

**Recommendation.** The kids who are in the room are the group. No minimum and no maximum.

## 3. DESIGN-DECISION lines used

**DESIGN-DECISION 1.** Three wake moments. “Starts to wake” is only the feather-trigger window (beat 2). “Wakes up” freezes every kid in a ridiculous pose (beats 1, 4, and 5, if the host calls it). “Opens their eyes” is the copycat statue for anyone still moving (beat 3). Used in H4, H5, H7, and the host card. “Ridiculous” is tone, so the pose is any still pose. “Copycat” uses the statue already written: one foot, finger on the nose. No feather prop.

**DESIGN-DECISION 2.** During the 5-second laugh, kids may move. That window is the exception. Outside it, a wake freezes them. Used in H4 and H9. The teacher spends those 5 seconds laughing (REQ-MEGA-011). The moving exception is the kids’. When the 5 seconds end, the teacher is asleep again. The end of the laugh is not itself a new wake.

**DESIGN-DECISION 3.** “The board” means the whiteboard. If the teacher is already facing it, the doodle window opens when they turn away and then turn back. If they are typing or reading, the doodle window opens when they turn to face the whiteboard. Used in H12 and the host card.

**DESIGN-DECISION 4.** Beat 10 wins Phase B. The session is won only after both finales. “Win the game” in beat 10 does not skip Phase A. Used in H14. Phase A is played first, so the mailbox sentence cannot skip it. The Phase B win is this decision, not a second session win.
