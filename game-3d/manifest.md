# Classroom 3D staging

Staged models only. The 2D game was not rebuilt.

All four packs are CC0 (Kay Lousberg / KayKit), taken from public GitHub releases of `atiffhussainkhan/Image`. Licence copies are in `licenses/`. Furniture glTF files are not self-contained: each `.gltf` needs its `.bin` and the shared `furniturebits_texture.png` in this same folder.

`atiffhussainkhan/animation` has no GLB or glTF. Its only catalog pack is the 2D knight / princess / dragon sheet, so nothing from that repo was copied.

The adventurer pack stores five rigged humanoids. Engineer and Druid appear only on poster sheets marked extra, with no mesh in the release. Knight is plate armor and was left out. Barbarian is bearded and was left out so the two kids are the two unbearded, unarmored rogue costumes. Mage, Rogue, and Rogue Hooded are the same chibi scale. This pack has no child-proportioned mesh.

## Named props with no model in these packs

Shoes, bubble wrap, squeaky toy, party popper, confetti, silly string, sticky note, clown sunglasses, mailbox, computer, laptop, whiteboard, trophy, and a separate feather. Boots exist only as part of the character meshes, not as a loose shoe. The animation pack’s pencil and case GLBs were left out because they are not those props.
